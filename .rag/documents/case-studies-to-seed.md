# Case studies to seed (historical analogues)

These are REAL events to seed your library with, so the chat can draw historical
parallels (the "Case Study" leg of ARVC). For each one, create a document file
(copy `_TEMPLATE.md`), then paste real text pulled from the linked primary
sources. Do not write the analysis yourself — pull it from real material.

| Event | Pull real text from |
|-------|---------------------|
| 2008 Global Financial Crisis | IMF Global Financial Stability Report 2008/09; Fed crisis timeline (federalreserve.gov) |
| 1997 Asian Financial Crisis | IMF "The Asian Crisis: Causes and Cures"; World Bank reports |
| 1991 India Balance-of-Payments crisis | RBI history archives; Ministry of Finance economic survey |
| 1971 Nixon Shock | Federal Reserve History (federalreservehistory.org) |
| 2010–2012 Eurozone debt crisis | ECB reports; IMF country reports (Greece, Portugal) |
| 1923 Weimar hyperinflation | Bundesbank historical material; academic primary sources |
| 2020 COVID-19 economic shock | IMF WEO April 2020; World Bank Global Economic Prospects |
| 2022 Russia sanctions & energy shock | IMF WEO Oct 2022; IEA energy reports |
| 1985 Plaza Accord | US Treasury / Fed historical records |
| 1979–82 Volcker disinflation | Federal Reserve History |
| Japan's "Lost Decade" (1990s) | Bank of Japan archives; IMF Japan reports |
| 2018 Turkey & Argentina currency crises | IMF country reports |

Tip: official institutions (IMF, World Bank, Fed, RBI) are ideal here — their
material is free, citable, and matches your "primary-source-only" positioning.
